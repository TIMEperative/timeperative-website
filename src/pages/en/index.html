What TIMEperative does

  • Record as you work

    Start the timer, take a break with one tap, or add entries by hand in a list, a week or a calendar. If you switch tracking on, it shows which apps, documents and calls filled your day, so you can turn them into entries.

  • Plan your weeks

    Plan by week and by day against your projects and their work breakdown, bring in your calendar, and compare what you planned with what you did.

  • Stay within the rules

    Checks against Swiss or German working-time law and your organisation's own rules show what needs a look before you send your hours.

  • Send it to your Cloud Hub

    Approved entries go to your projects in the TIMEperative Cloud Hub with your own login, and your projects and tasks come back the same way. The Cloud Hub needs the separate TIMEperative.sync app.

Your data stays on your device

There is no TIMEperative account and no server of ours. Your entries live in a database on your computer or phone. Apart from the update check, nothing leaves it unless you connect a calendar, translate with DeepL or send entries to your TIMEperative Cloud Hub.
