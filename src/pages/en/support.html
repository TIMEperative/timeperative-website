Support

Questions, a problem or an idea? Write to us at {{support.email}}. Your message reaches the people who build TIMEperative, and we answer from the same address.

What helps us help you

  • The version you use: App Settings, Version & updates.
  • Windows or Android, and which version of it.
  • What you did, what you expected and what happened instead.
  • A screenshot, if it shows the problem. Hide names, customers and anything else that is not ours to see.

Before you write

  • Check for updates in App Settings, Version & updates: the problem may be fixed already. The release notes say what changed.
  • Windows warns about the installer the first time: Download explains why and what to do.
  • Your data stays on your device, so we cannot see your entries or restore them. Export your entries now and then while TIMEperative is in alpha.

Privacy questions
