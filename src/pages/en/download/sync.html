TIMEperative.sync

TIMEperative.sync is installed once on your organisation's TIMEperative Cloud Hub. From then on, everyone's approved entries reach your projects, and your projects and tasks reach the app.

Ask for access

  • What it does

    • Receives the entries people send from the app, with their own login
    • Lets team leads approve them before they count
    • Gives the app your projects, tasks and organisation settings

  • What you need

    • A TIMEperative Cloud Hub on series 20.0, run by your organisation
    • Someone who may install apps on it
    • A login on the Cloud Hub for each person who sends entries

  • How you get it

    • Ask for access with the form below
    • We send you the licence agreement
    • Once it is signed, you get the release files and the installation guide
