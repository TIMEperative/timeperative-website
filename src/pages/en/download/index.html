Before you install

Windows asks once

The installer is not code-signed yet. Windows may therefore show “Windows protected your PC” the first time. If you downloaded the file from this page, choose More info, then Run anyway. Where Smart App Control is on, or where your organisation blocks unsigned apps, Windows does not offer that choice; ask your IT team, or wait for the signed release.

Android asks once

The first time you open the APK, Android asks whether your browser may install apps. Allow it for this installation; you can switch it off again afterwards in the phone's settings. TIMEperative on Google Play and in the Microsoft Store comes later.

Early access

TIMEperative is an alpha release. It keeps your data in a database on your device, and each update migrates it and keeps snapshots of the last versions. Still, export your entries now and then while the app is in alpha.
