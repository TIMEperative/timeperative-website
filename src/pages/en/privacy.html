The TIMEperative app

Where your data is stored

Everything you record lives in one database on your device: on Windows in the app's folder in your user's local application data (%LOCALAPPDATA%), which does not roam with your profile; on Android in the app's private storage. TIMEperative does not encrypt this database itself, so protect your device with its own encryption, such as BitLocker on Windows.

Passwords, keys and sign-in tokens are kept in the system's secure store: the Windows Credential Manager, or on Android under a key in the Android Keystore. Two kinds of settings are kept in the database instead: the client ID and secret of your own Google registration, and calendar links you paste in.

Before an update changes the database, the app keeps a copy of it on your device; it keeps the last five. On Android, the app is excluded from cloud backups, so its data is not copied to your Google account. If you delete the app's data, nobody can restore it, not even we.

What the app records

The app stores what you enter: your time entries, projects, plans, trips, budgets and settings. Tracking is off until you switch it on. When it is on, it records on your device only:

  • on Windows, which app and window title was in front, every three seconds, with a pause after four minutes without input; your browser history, read from the browser's own file; recently used documents; and when the PC was in use, from the Windows system log;
  • on Windows, when Microsoft Teams or Zoom last used the microphone, read from Windows' own privacy settings: the app, the start and the end, nothing of the call itself;
  • on Android, how long you used which apps, with Android's usage access, which you grant yourself, and pages you share to TIMEperative from your browser.

The app never reads the conversations of AI assistants, and it uses no location services: it asks for no location permission and reads no GPS.

What you control

  • Switch tracking on or off at any time, hide window titles, and exclude websites.
  • Dismiss or delete what tracking found. Deleting keeps only a short fingerprint and the time span, so the same item is not suggested again.
  • Clear tracking per period, delete and restore entries, and purge them for good.
  • Export your entries as CSV, and the configuration as a file without entries and without secrets.

This website

Cloudflare, Inc. (United States) hosts timeperative.com. To deliver the pages and protect them against attacks, Cloudflare processes your IP address, the address you request and what your browser sends with it, possibly outside Switzerland, including in the United States.
